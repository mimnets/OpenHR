OpenHRApp is free. Not free-for-now, not free-until-you-grow — there is no paid plan, no per-employee price, and no feature locked behind an upgrade. What this page explains is how the project is funded instead, and what the subscription states in your organization settings actually mean.

Is there a paid version?

No. Every feature is available to every organization at no cost, with no employee limit. The project is open source and funded two ways: advertising, shown inside the app, and voluntary donations, which remove those ads for the organization that gives.

That is the whole commercial model. If you never donate and never think about it again, the app keeps working.

What the subscription states mean

  • Trial — your opening period, during which no ads are shown. Despite the name, nothing expires into a bill.
  • Ad Supported — the ordinary long-term state. Every feature works; ad banners appear in the app.
  • Active — ad-free, granted after a donation.
  • Expired — read-only. Records can be viewed but not created or changed.
  • Suspended — no access at all.

The last two are administrative states applied deliberately by the OpenHRApp team, not something an ordinary organization drifts into. A normal organization only ever sees the first three.

What happens at the end of the ad-free period?

You move to Ad Supported. Ads begin appearing, and that is the entire change — no feature is switched off, no data becomes read-only, no employee limit appears, and nothing needs to be paid.

Your data is never held hostage in any state. Everything you have entered stays yours and stays exportable.

Your three options

All three live under Upgrade, available to Admin and HR.

Option 1 — Donate, and go ad-free

A donation removes ads for your whole organization. Four levels:

  • $5 — 3 months ad-free
  • $10 — 6 months
  • $20 — 1 year
  • $50 — lifetime

Donations go through Buy Me a Coffee. Because that happens outside the app, OpenHRApp cannot see your payment automatically — so after donating you submit a request with a screenshot of the receipt, and the team switches your organization to Active once it is checked. It is a manual step, and it is why the receipt matters.

Option 2 — Accept ad-supported

Switches your organization to Ad Supported immediately. This one is self-service — no request, no approval, no waiting. Choose it and it is done.

It is also where organizations end up by default, so choosing it early simply removes the question.

Option 3 — Request more ad-free time

If you are still evaluating and would rather not see ads yet, you can request an extension of the ad-free period with a short reason. This one is reviewed by the OpenHRApp team rather than granted automatically, so allow a little time.

What do the ads look like?

Ads appear on public-facing pages and inside the app for organizations in the Ad Supported state. They do not appear on sign-in, and they are never mixed into your employee data, reports or exports.

If ads are unacceptable for your context — a clinic, a school, a government office — the donation route removes them permanently for $50, which is the intended answer for exactly that situation.

Common questions

Will I lose my data if I do nothing?

No. Doing nothing moves you to Ad Supported and everything keeps working.

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. There is no card on file at any point, because there is nothing to charge.

I donated but still see ads

Check that you submitted the request with your receipt screenshot — the donation alone is invisible to the app. If you did, it is waiting on review.

Can I donate more than once?

Yes, and the lifetime tier exists so you do not have to. Repeat donations from organizations that are already ad-free are what keep the project running for everyone who never donates.

Does donating unlock features?

No — there are none to unlock. It removes ads and supports the work. Any page suggesting a feature is available only to donors is out of date; please report it.

Is it read-only after the free period?

No. Read-only is the Expired state, which is applied deliberately by the OpenHRApp team, not something the passage of time causes.
