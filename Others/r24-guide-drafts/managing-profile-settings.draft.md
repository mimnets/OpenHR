# Managing Your Profile and Settings

Your profile is where OpenHRApp keeps who you are: the details that appear next to your name across the app, the shift your attendance is measured against, and the password you sign in with. Some of it you control, and some of it your organization controls — knowing which is which saves a lot of hunting for a field that will not edit.

Open **My Profile** from the sidebar to find all of it. Administrators and HR see the same screen listed a second time as **Settings** — it is the same page either way.

## What can I change myself?

Two fields, and they cover most of what people need:

- **Full Name** — how you appear everywhere in the app: attendance records, leave requests, the employee directory, notifications sent to your manager.
- **Work Email** — where OpenHRApp writes to you, and the address you sign in with.

Edit either, then save. Changing your name updates it everywhere it already appears, including on records created before the change — there is no history of previous names, so a legal name change does not leave old documents inconsistent.

## How do I change my profile photo?

You cannot set it yourself. Your photo is shown at the top of your profile and beside your name in the employee directory, but uploading one is done by an administrator or HR through the employee record, not from this screen.

If you have no photo, OpenHRApp shows the first letter of your name instead, so nothing looks broken while you wait. To get a photo added, ask whoever manages your organization's directory — see [managing employees](/how-to-use/managing-employees).

## Why can't I edit my employee ID or shift?

Because those describe your *employment*, not your identity, and letting people set them would make the records built on them meaningless. Four fields are shown read-only:

- **Official Employee ID** — assigned by your organization. Shows *Not Assigned* if nobody has set one.
- **Reporting To** — your line manager. Shows *No Direct Manager* if none is set. This determines who approves your leave and who can see your attendance.
- **Team** — the team you belong to, which controls which colleagues you can see in the directory.
- **Assigned Shift** — your shift with its start and end times, or *No Shift Assigned*. This is the single most important read-only field, because it is what "late" is measured against.

If any of these is wrong, an administrator or HR can change it — see [managing employees](/how-to-use/managing-employees). It is worth checking your assigned shift early rather than after a month of attendance has been recorded against the wrong one.

## How do I change my password?

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Find the password section.
3. Enter your **current password**, then the new one.
4. Save.

Your current password is required, so someone who finds your machine unlocked still cannot lock you out of your own account. If you have forgotten it, do not use this form — sign out and use the reset link on the sign-in page, which sends a link to your work email.

### Choosing a password worth having

Your OpenHRApp account can see colleagues' contact details and, depending on your role, the whole organization's attendance. Treat it accordingly: use a password you have not used anywhere else, prefer length over complexity — a passphrase of three or four unrelated words beats a short string of symbols — and store it in a password manager rather than a note.

## Choosing light or dark

OpenHRApp has three display settings:

- **Light** — light background, dark text.
- **Dark** — dark background, light text. Easier in low light.
- **System** — follow whatever your device is set to, and switch automatically when your device does.

The choice applies immediately, with no page reload, and is remembered the next time you sign in on that browser.

One thing that catches people out: this preference is stored **on the device**, not on your account. Setting dark mode on your laptop does not set it on your phone, and it is not carried between browsers. That is deliberate — it means the app can apply your choice before the first pixel is drawn, so you never get a flash of the wrong theme while it waits for the network. The trade-off is that you set it once per device.

Clearing your browser's site data resets it to System, which is also the default for anyone who has never chosen.

## Your notification preferences

Notification settings do not live on this screen. Open the **notification bell** in the top bar to mute categories you do not want — announcements, leave, attendance, reviews, system — and to set how often you receive email.

Your organization decides which categories exist and can turn some off for everyone. You can silence anything that remains, but you cannot switch on a category your administrator has disabled. See [notification settings](/how-to-use/notification-settings) for how the two levels interact.

## Getting help

The **Contact Support** section at the bottom of the page is the direct route for anything this guide does not cover. Include what you were doing and what you expected to happen — for attendance questions in particular, the specific date is usually all a support answer needs.

Administrators have an additional **Admin Tools** section on this screen that other roles do not see, covering verification tasks for the organization rather than personal settings.
